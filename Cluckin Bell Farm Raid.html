This Mission takes inclusive preps and final around 60 minutes. It pays $500.000
You do not need any property to buy to start this Mission.
Its recommended to do all missions on Easy

Preps missions

Slush Fund

Go in the 2 buildings and grep all the money in the was machines.

Breaking and Entering

Recommend to do this in order:

Laptop
Steal a laptop. (Tip to use the Vigilante with homing missiles. Where the missile is locked on there is the laptop.)
Hacking Device
Steal a Hacking device in the Terrorbyte.
Train
First kill the enemies there it will drop a key. Then you can drive the train.

Weapons Concealed

Steal Weapons and armor for the final.
Recommend:
B: Professional

Hit and Run

Steal a Gate a way vehicle
Recommend:
B: Professional

Disorganized Crime

This decides the finale is in stealth or in aggressive.

Stealth

Complete all of these steps to activate stealth mode.

Enamies: kill them all stealthy
Enamies: if spotted kill them all before the time is up.
No trucks are destroyed
All trucks are sabotaged
Disable CCTV

When you leave the building and this message popup in your screen then you will have activated Stealth mode everything else will be Aggressive.
