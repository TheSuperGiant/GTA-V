First you need to steal cargo and paying for it or let your Staff Member source cargo for you. on the map there is an human icon. This takes 1 GTA day (48 minutes). Sometimes you can source special cargo you will get more money when selling for these.
The Staff Member will sometimes source special cargo.
After you have done this and you found now its the right moment to sell it you can start a sell mission then i recommended to sell all crates at once. for the sell mission you can get planes boats or cars. There will always drop multiple vehicles.
