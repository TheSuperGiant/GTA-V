With the Salvage Yard you will get 5 stealing car Robberies (heists).
You can do 3 of them every weak.
Sometimes there is a claimable car that you can hold for your self for only $20,000.
There is even an tow truck that is possible to do this mission you must go out on the map to pickup a car and bring it back to the Salvage Yard. Earnings between $20,000 and $25,000.
There is an passive income of $300-$24,000 per GTA day (48 minutes) based on how active you do the tow truck mission.
