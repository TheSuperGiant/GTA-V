Passive Income

There is an passive income of $500-$50.000 per GTA day (48 minutes) based on the popularity of your Nightclub.
There are a couple ways to increase your popularity:
Blue Dot - If you see it then you can trow out a drunk costumer to get 25% popularity increase.
Blue Man - If you see it then you can do an mission that you must bring a VIP to a location driving slowly to get 25% popularity increase.
Promote Club - on the computer on the tab Nightclub Management can you select a mission to ...% popularity increase.
Hire new DJ - Cost 100K: You need to a pickup mission and 100% popularity increase.
Hire a DJ again - Cost 10K: 10% popularity increase
Dancing - Dance in the Nightclub will increase your popularity
